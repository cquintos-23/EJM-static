FAQ

As of 2026, we have tracked 699 eco jurisprudence initiatives in 67 countries and territories. 523 of them are rights of nature. Yes, there is a difference. Check out our initiative index to explore the diversity of the movement.

We track all kinds of ecocentric law around the world. In addition to the rights of nature, we monitor environmental personhood, animal rights, ecological governance systems, Indigenous models, local ecological knowledge, and ecocide. Our codebook has all the definitions you need.

For every initiative, we try to gather as much information as possible: who, what, when, where, how, and why? That means a lot of research that leads to many exciting rabbit holes. Have you ever heard about the disputed Indonesian province of West Papua and its Indigenous Green Philosophy Declaration? Well, now you do.

We are doing our very best to be the most reliable and comprehensive resource on ecocentric law globally. We are also humans and make errors. If you find one, please let us know and we will fix them as quickly as we can. Is an initiative missing? Please tell us that too. We rely on our community to build this resource with and for the public. Thank you!
